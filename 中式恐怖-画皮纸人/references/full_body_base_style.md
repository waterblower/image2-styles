# Full Body Base Style

Full figures should read as weight-bearing silhouettes made from layered veils, paper sheets, gauze, and diluted ink stains. Keep the body structure legible under translucent material: shoulder slope, ribcage, pelvis, elbow, wrist, knee, and center of gravity should still be present even when softened.

Clothing or body coverings behave like crumpled rice paper: broad folded planes, torn translucent hems, fibrous wrinkles, cloudy gray-beige staining, and thin branchlike crease lines. The silhouette can trail into ribbonlike sheets, but it should not become random smoke.

Use elongated, graceful proportions with restrained motion. Avoid superhero anatomy, fashion-render gloss, hard cloth simulation, and heavy opaque fabric.
