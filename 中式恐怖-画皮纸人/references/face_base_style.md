# Face Base Style

Faces are porcelain-pale and softly simplified, with quiet closed or lowered expressions, reduced feature contrast, and a thin veil of milky translucency over the skin. Eyelids, brows, nose bridge, lips, and jawline are indicated by diluted ink shadows and barely-there contour marks rather than crisp anatomy. Skin should keep subtle volume but feel like translucent paper or unfired porcelain with internal fiber shadows.

Hair is grouped into soft dark smoke masses with loose stray strands, dry brush edges, and threadlike ink lines. Avoid shiny hair, strand-perfect realism, or clean anime clumps.

Facial edges should be feathered and atmospheric: cheek, chin, ears, and hairline can dissolve into pale wash, but the expression must remain readable.
