# Shared Style Invariants

Macro medium: `2d_3d_hybrid`.

Evidence: the references combine flat ink-drawing contour logic, watercolor-like tonal stains, and fibrous paper texture with sculptural translucent volume, veiled edges, and photographed-object depth. The look should not become anime painting, glossy 3D render, or ordinary photorealism.

Base style:
translucent handmade rice-paper and diluted ink-wash art style, hybrid of fragile paper sculpture, pale mineral watercolor, and fine hand-drawn contour drawing; layered semi-transparent fibrous sheets, wrinkled vellum edges, dry plant-fiber veins, faint graphite and ink hairline marks, soft eroded silhouettes, misty low-contrast forms, porcelain-pale faces or surfaces, muted ivory, bone white, ash gray, tea-stained beige, smoke blue-gray, faint olive shadow, occasional tiny desaturated red accent; details appear as embedded fibers, cracks, wrinkles, and threadlike ink traces rather than sharp rendered texture; edges are feathered, torn, folded, or veil-like; forms feel weightless but still have readable sculptural volume.

Transferable fingerprints:
- Pale desaturated palette with almost no saturated color.
- Translucent layers reveal internal veins, wrinkles, and grey-green ink stains.
- Contours are thin, wandering, and handmade, never clean vector outlines.
- Highlights are milky and matte, with preserved tonal detail.
- Shadows are broad, cool, dusty, and low contrast.
- Figures and objects should look assembled from paper, gauze, ink, and dried fibers.
- Finish is quiet, archaic, fragile, and museum-like, not glossy or commercial.

Avoid content leakage:
- Do not require ancient Chinese costumes, a specific tree, a painter, a brush, calligraphy, a seated figure, or any source-image composition.
- Use those only when the user asks for similar content.
