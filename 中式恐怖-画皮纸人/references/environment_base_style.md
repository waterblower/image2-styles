# Environment Base Style

Spaces are pale, hushed, and low contrast, as if built from handmade paper relief, chalky plaster, fog, and diluted ink. Natural or architectural forms should have soft sculptural mass with fibrous surface marks and muted tonal staining.

Background detail density is moderate to low: readable planes first, texture second. Use layered mist, faded outlines, and paper-grain surfaces to separate foreground, middle ground, and distance.

Foliage, ground, walls, and distant forms should share the same ivory, ash, tea-stain, and smoke-gray palette. Avoid lush green foliage, photographic landscape detail, hard perspective grids, or saturated atmospheric effects.
