# Face Base Style

## Positive Constraints

Use realistic human facial proportions with natural feature scale, grounded facial anatomy, and soft but structurally clear facial planes. Keep the rendering painterly, with broad blended brushwork, subtle oil/canvas texture, and restrained pore and eyelid detail.

Skin should be matte to satin with controlled subsurface color, fine pore texture, and soft diffuse transitions. Eye and lip wetness may appear only as tiny controlled accents, never as glossy glare. Hair near the face should be simplified into larger soft masses with sparse loose strokes, low strand count, and minimal reflection.

Lighting should remain global and soft: broad diffused illumination, natural bounce, soft shadow transitions, controlled highlight rolloff, and preserved color detail in the brightest facial planes.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid anime eyes, doll face, chibi proportions, Q-style proportions, manga/comic facial rendering, waxy skin, oily skin, plastic skin, greasy lips, strong forehead hotspots, sharp rim highlights, glossy hair ribbons, wet specular hair, excessive individual hair strands, hard lineart, and beauty-filter smoothness.
