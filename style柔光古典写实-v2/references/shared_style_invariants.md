# Shared Style Invariants

## Embedded Base Style

semi-realistic painterly illustration, softened 3D rendering, porcelain-smooth airbrushed planes, translucent layered brush blending, muted and faded palette, low-to-medium contrast, broad soft shadow masses with feathered edges, matte refined surfaces with only small controlled satin highlights, wispy details rendered as soft painted shapes, subtle paper-like grain, high-detail focal areas with quiet simplified peripheral detail

## Positive Constraints

These constraints refine the embedded base style above; keep that base style intact and resolve any ambiguity in favor of the embedded base style.

Classical realist painterly digital oil illustration with semi-realistic painterly construction and softened 3D-rendered volume. Keep the image grounded in realistic anatomy, believable volume, and natural material behavior, while retaining visible brushwork, soft blended planes, and subtle canvas/oil texture.

Use clean wide-gamut color with separated warm-cool relationships. Preserve the classical atmosphere without an antique dark-yellow cast, muddy sepia wash, or monochrome brown grading. Let local colors remain fresh and readable under the soft lighting.

Use global soft illumination, broad diffused area light, natural bounced light, low-to-medium contrast, controlled highlight rolloff, and preserved color detail in bright areas. Shadows should be soft, broad, and atmospheric rather than hard or theatrical.

Use restrained detail frequency. Primary forms are clear, but secondary background surfaces, ornament, and texture stay simplified. Edges should be soft and hierarchical: clearer at focal forms, feathered and painterly away from the focus.

Skin, hair, fabric, and hard materials should read as painterly materials rather than glossy CG. Skin stays matte to satin. Hair is grouped into larger masses with sparse strand indications and minimal reflection. Background environments use broad planes, low ornament density, and low surface detail.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid dark yellow antique cast, muddy sepia palette, heavy brown wash, overexposed highlights, blown-out whites, oily wet shine, greasy specular reflections, point-light hotspots, sparkle pollution, harsh flash lighting, uncontrolled glossy reflections, glossy plastic hair, wet specular hair, excessive hair strand detail, hyper-detailed background, photorealistic PBR 3D render, anime eyes, doll face, chibi proportions, Q-style proportions, manga rendering, comic rendering, hard vector lineart, and decorative high-shine effects.
