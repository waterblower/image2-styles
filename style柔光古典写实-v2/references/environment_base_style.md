# Environment Base Style

## Positive Constraints

Embedded base style: semi-realistic painterly illustration, softened 3D rendering, porcelain-smooth airbrushed planes, translucent layered brush blending, muted and faded palette, low-to-medium contrast, broad soft shadow masses with feathered edges, matte refined surfaces with only small controlled satin highlights, wispy details rendered as soft painted shapes, subtle paper-like grain, high-detail focal areas with quiet simplified peripheral detail

Use classical realist painterly environment rendering with broad readable spatial planes, restrained detail density, soft atmospheric depth, and subtle oil/canvas texture. Large forms should read clearly while surface detail stays low to moderate.

Use clean wide-gamut color organization with separated warm-cool relationships and preserved local color. Avoid a single antique warm wash. Surfaces should use matte mineral and natural material responses, subtle microtexture, low reflection, and simplified ornament.

Lighting should use global soft illumination, broad diffused source quality, natural bounce, soft contact shadows, controlled highlight rolloff, and preserved color in bright areas. Keep highlights broad and atmospheric, not hard or spot-like.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid antique color cast, muddy old-paint palette, hard light patches, point-light hotspots, mirror-like floor glare, uncontrolled glossy reflections, overexposed bright areas, dense tiny ornament, hyper-detailed background, cluttered small props, photographic 3D render look, anime/manga/comic background rendering, and hard vector edges.
