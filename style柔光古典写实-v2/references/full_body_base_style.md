# Full Body Base Style

## Positive Constraints

Use realistic full-body proportions, believable anatomy, visible body volume, grounded weight, natural balance, and clear gesture force. Limbs should have convincing structure under soft painterly planes rather than flattened decorative silhouettes.

Clothing should follow the body and gravity. Use broad fabric folds, soft tension points, and restrained seam or edge detail. Skin, hair, fabric, leather, and hard accessories should keep separate material responses while staying unified by the same broad soft illumination.

Use painterly body edges: firm enough to read the silhouette, softer in secondary contours. Keep detail concentrated at the important structural areas and reduce background detail around the figure.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid chibi proportions, Q-style proportions, manga/comic anatomy, elongated fashion sketch distortion, broken joints, weak weight bearing, flattened limbs, floating pose, fabric that ignores the body, oily skin shine, glossy plastic hair, excessive hair strand detail, harsh rim light, and over-detailed background.
