# Glass Material

## Positive Constraints

Embedded base style: semi-realistic painterly illustration, softened 3D rendering, porcelain-smooth airbrushed planes, translucent layered brush blending, muted and faded palette, low-to-medium contrast, broad soft shadow masses with feathered edges, matte refined surfaces with only small controlled satin highlights, wispy details rendered as soft painted shapes, subtle paper-like grain, high-detail focal areas with quiet simplified peripheral detail

Transparent glass with softened refraction, muted edge highlights, subtle internal tint, and broad painterly strokes. Use highlights as controlled accents only.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid glittering caustics, hard white streaks, blown-out edges, mirror glare, noisy sparkle, and photorealistic ray-traced glass.
