# Shared Style Invariants

Macro medium: pure 3D render.

Base style: stylized high-end 3D render, pure CG / game cinematic render, PBR material response, ray-traced cinematic lighting, real camera depth of field, clean contact shadows, polished pre-rendered cutscene finish, selective sharpness on the subject or foreground material with softened background edges.

Shape language: idealized but physically dimensional forms, softened rounded silhouettes, clean readable primary geometry, controlled secondary details, refined planes rather than noisy realism.

Lighting grammar: soft diffused cinematic daylight, warm highlight rolloff, cool pale ambient fill, low-to-medium contrast, clear contact shadows, restrained specular accents, no harsh black crush.

Color system: cream and pale blue base, warm rose skin or accent tones, muted gold highlights, moss or stone neutrals when environment materials are present. Saturation is controlled and pastel-leaning, with clean CG postprocess and gentle highlight rolloff.

Detail frequency: high-end PBR detail is visible but restrained. Skin pores, hair strands, fabric weave, stone pits, scratches, patina, and water droplets should be legible without becoming noisy or hyper-photographic.

Finish boundary: pre-rendered game cutscene / premium CG still, not 2D illustration, not painterly concept art, not live-action photography, not generic glossy anime.
