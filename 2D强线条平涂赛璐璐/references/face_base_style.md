# Face Base Style

Use youthful simplified manga proportions: large expressive eyes, compact nose, clean mouth shapes, sharp but stylized jaw and cheek planes. Skin uses warm peach broad planes with one or two large shadow blocks, not pores or painterly texture.

Eyes use layered iris color, dark upper lash mass, thin lower lash accents, and two controlled catchlights with preserved color detail. Hair near the face is built from large ribbon-like locks with thick outer lines, tapered interior lines, and selective crisp highlights following strand direction.

Facial edges are clearest at the jaw, eyelids, hair silhouette, mouth interior, and cast-shadow boundary. Detail frequency stays high only around eyes, lashes, hair overlaps, and mouth; cheeks, forehead, neck, and plain background remain quiet smooth fields.
