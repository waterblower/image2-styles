# Full Body Base Style

Use simplified but lively anime body construction with readable weight, limb tension, and clear center of gravity. Body volume is expressed by large angular skin and clothing planes, not realistic anatomy rendering.

Clothing must follow body pressure, joints, seams, and weight-bearing folds. Use broad fold shapes with a few functional seam, belt, buckle, zipper, or cuff details. Avoid dense accessories, all-over ornaments, or repeated filler patterns.

Materials remain separated by value and highlight width: skin is warm and smooth, hair is saturated with ribbon-like locks, matte fabric uses broad dark folds, leather uses controlled satin bands, and metal uses dull edge highlights. Full figure silhouettes should be strong and readable from a distance.
