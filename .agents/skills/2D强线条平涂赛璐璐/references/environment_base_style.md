# Environment Base Style

Use simplified urban or interior spaces made from large wall, floor, sign, glass, metal, and shadow planes. Architecture has thick outer contour lines with thinner interior construction lines only where panels, vents, signs, seams, and perspective edges need clarity.

The environment should feel graphic and readable: muted teal-gray shadows, cream walls, dark green framing, and restrained warm orange signs or labels. Use bold cast-shadow shapes and diagonal poster-like cropping.

Keep distant and middle-ground details quiet. Do not fill walls, pavement, foliage, vents, or windows with repeated fine marks. Detail belongs at functional panel edges, printed sign line groups, hinges, handles, vents, drains, and contact shadows.
