# Shared Style Invariants

Macro medium: `pure_2d`. The style is a clean modern manga-cover digital illustration, not 3D render, photography, watercolor, gouache, paper illustration, or vintage print.

Core style:
- Thick dark outer contours with thinner tapered interior lines.
- Smooth broad color fields and quiet midtone areas.
- Flat anime cel-painting with soft airbrushed transitions only inside large shadow shapes.
- Graphic, poster-like diagonal energy and cropped framing when appropriate.
- Precise material separation by line hierarchy, value blocks, and controlled highlight width.
- Warm saturated orange/coral accents balanced by muted teal-gray, dark green, cream, and peach planes.
- Strong cast-shadow shapes, especially across faces, walls, objects, and clothing planes.
- Selective crisp highlights only on focal eyes, hair ribbons, dull metal edges, glass edges, lips, or controlled glossy surfaces.
- Structural detail only: seams, folds, panel edges, printed line groups, vents, hinges, buckles, and leaf veins must explain form or function.
- Backgrounds and middle areas stay simplified with large readable shapes and clean negative space.

Surface language:
- Visible brushwork: low.
- Paper or canvas grain: absent.
- Global noise or speckle: absent.
- Texture distribution: none by default, localized only when material structure requires it.
- Value transition: softened blocks, not dense painterly strokes.
- Material separation: precise.
- Highlight design: painted selective, never oily or sparkle-heavy.

Do not inherit source content: orange hair, green eye, newspaper, word shapes, collar, open mouth, crop, outfit, or identity are not required unless the user asks for similar content.
